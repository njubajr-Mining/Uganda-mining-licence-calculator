⚠ Legal Requirement — Value Addition & Export Restrictions
Under the Mining and Minerals Act, 2022 and government policy, export of unprocessed / raw minerals is banned. All minerals must undergo value addition before export. For gold specifically:
  • Raw or unrefined gold cannot legally be exported.
  • Gold must be refined to a minimum purity of 99.9% before export.
  • Refining must be done at a licensed refinery (e.g. African Gold Refinery, Simba Gold Refinery, Bullion Gold Refinery).
  • You must hold a valid Mineral Dealer's Licence (precious metals) or a Mineral Refining Licence.
  • Gold trading may only occur between holders of valid licences — unlicensed trading is a criminal offence under the Act.
  • Uganda is a signatory to the ICGLR Mineral Tracking & Certification Scheme — chain-of-custody documentation is required for gold, cassiterite, wolframite and coltan.

Export permits are issued per consignment under the Mining and Minerals (Licensing) Regulations, 2023. Each permit has a fixed fee of UGX 700,000 plus a variable component by mineral class. Gold also attracts a separate URA Export Levy of USD 200 per kilogram on refined gold, paid to URA at export declaration.

Gold export levy: USD 200 per kg · Mining and Minerals (Export Levy on Refined Gold) Regulations, 2023, revised May 2024 · Purity: 99.9% minimum · Paid to URA separately from DGSM permit.
